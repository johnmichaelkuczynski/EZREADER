The quarterly report came in over the weekend and the numbers were worse than anyone expected. Revenue fell to $4,210,000 against a forecast of $4,800,000, a shortfall of nearly $590,000 that the finance team had not flagged in either of the two prior reviews.

The hardware line was the largest drag. Average selling price slipped from $899 to $749 across the flagship SKU, and the accessory bundle that used to clear at $129 now moved at $99 only after a $30 promotional discount. Margin per unit fell from $312 to $187, a 40% compression that the manufacturing team attributes to component cost inflation of roughly $42 per unit since the prior quarter.

On the operating side, payroll grew by $310,000 quarter-over-quarter, primarily driven by the new sales hires who carry an average loaded cost of $185,000 each. Office lease renewal came in $48,000 higher than the previous year. Cloud spend rose by $22,500. Travel, which had been budgeted at $75,000, came in at $112,000, mostly attributable to the regional conference in March which alone cost $38,500 between airfare, hotel, and the $4,200 booth fee.

We are recommending a $1.2 million correction to next quarter's plan, a $300,000 reduction in discretionary marketing, and a freeze on any new hires whose loaded cost would exceed $150,000. The price is $99 for the accessory bundle going forward, with no further discount. Costs of $200 or more require executive approval. Customers will see the $5.99 monthly subscription price hold flat through the end of the year.
